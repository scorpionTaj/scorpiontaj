## 🚀 About Me

I am a passionate Data Scientist and AI enthusiast currently advancing my expertise through Master's studies in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at the Faculté des sciences de Meknès.   

My background spans machine learning, big data analytics, and full-stack development. I have a demonstrated ability to transform complex datasets into actionable insights through innovative ML solutions, ranging from real-time prediction systems to intelligent document processing. I love building production-ready pipelines and have hands-on experience creating unified LLM gateways and end-to-end predictive maintenance systems.   

I am driven by combining technical proficiency with strategic thinking to drive impactful solutions. I also deeply value community leadership, having served as a Board Member for the Moroccan Data Scientists (MDS) to promote data science knowledge and open data initiatives across Morocco.   

When I'm not coding:

I focus on personal fitness through bodybuilding and swimming.   
I love to explore new places through travel.   
I am multilingual, speaking Arabic, English, and French.
